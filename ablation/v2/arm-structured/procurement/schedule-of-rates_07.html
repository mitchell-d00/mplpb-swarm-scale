Schedule-Of-Rates (procurement)

A amendment is recorded as it happens rather than reconstructed afterwards, and the record names the operator.

The schedule-of-rates step is owned by the team named in scope and is not delegated once begun.

Where quotation and novation disagree the narrower declared scope decides and the wider defers.

The practice below is written to stand on its own. Where a decision depends on a condition the condition is stated rather than implied, because an implied condition is one that every reader reconstructs differently. It is revised when it stops matching what people do.
