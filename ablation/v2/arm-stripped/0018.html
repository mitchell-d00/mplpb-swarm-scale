Retention-Window (monitoring)

Where threshold and aggregation disagree the narrower declared scope decides and the wider defers.

The retention-window step is owned by the team named in scope and is not delegated once begun.

The practice below is written to stand on its own. Where a decision depends on a condition the condition is stated rather than implied, because an implied condition is one that every reader reconstructs differently. It is revised when it stops matching what people do.

A histogram is recorded as it happens rather than reconstructed afterwards, and the record names the operator.
